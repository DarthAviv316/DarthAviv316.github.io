Alphabetiles – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Alphabetiles ("the Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by Aviv316 ("we", "us").

In short

  • The Game has no accounts and no servers of our own. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress, records and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob. To do so, Google collects some device information, such as the advertising ID and IP address.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following only on your device: your unfinished games, scores and records, daily challenge results, your settings (sound, theme, board size, letter style) and which items you have bought. This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game. Note that deleting it also deletes your progress.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), operated by Google LLC. According to Google, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device;
  • Interactions with the Game and the ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • Diagnostic information about app and SDK performance, such as crash logs and launch time;
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. We only receive aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown). The Game asks for family-friendly ads only.

The Game shows two kinds of ads: an optional ad you can choose to watch for more undos, a restart or extra attempts at a past daily challenge, and an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a finished game. Buying "Remove ads" turns all ads off.

More about how Google uses this information:

  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Your choices about ads

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message when the Game first starts. You can change your choice at any time with the shield button in the Game's settings.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings › Google › Ads). After deleting it, ads may still appear, but they will not be personalized using that ID.
  • Remove ads: the in-game purchase "Remove ads" stops all ads.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (extra languages and "Remove ads") are processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name, email or payment details; the Game only receives a confirmation of which items were bought, so it can unlock them.

5. Sharing your results

If you tap a share button, the Game opens your device's share menu with a short text about your result. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to share it with.

6. What we do not collect

We do not collect or ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. The Game has no sign-in, no chat and no analytics service of its own.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information on your device stays there until you clear the Game's storage or uninstall it. Information collected by Google through ads is kept according to Google's own policies; you can manage it with the tools described in section 3 and in your Google account.

8. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not store personal data on our side, most requests concern data held by Google, which you can manage through Google's tools. You are always welcome to contact us with any question or request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
